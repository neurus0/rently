- `workshop-drill.jpg` — Sean, “A person holding a cordless drill in their hand”
- `camera.jpg` — ShareGrid, “Black DSLR camera”
- `camera-studio.jpg` — Indra Projects, “A black canon DSLR camera on a white background”
- `gardening.jpg` — feey, “Gardening tools and plants create a tranquil outdoor scene”

They are used as a compact, category-led local asset set. Replacing or expanding individual product images later only requires adding a local file and updating the seeded image array; components do not contain product-specific paths.
